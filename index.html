Welcome

A clinic built aroundthe person, not the treatment.

LR MediClinic brings aesthetic treatments, advanced skin services and private blood testing together in one calm, professional setting in Sidmouth. Some people arrive with a specific treatment in mind. Others simply want a clear, honest conversation about what might help.

How it works

Four steps, fromchoice to results.

  1. 01

    Choose your test

    Review the testing categories listed here, or ask the clinic which panel fits what you would like to know.

  2. 02

    Book your appointment

    Appointments are booked through the clinic's booking platform or by calling the clinic directly.

  3. 03

    Sample drawn at the clinic

    Your blood sample is taken at LR MediClinic in Sidmouth by the clinic's practitioner.

  4. 04

    Tested by Randox Health

    The sample is sent to Randox Health for testing. Results and any follow-up questions are handled by the clinic.
