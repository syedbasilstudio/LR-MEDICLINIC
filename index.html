Welcome

A clinic built aroundthe person, not the treatment.

LR MediClinic brings aesthetic treatments, advanced skin services and wellness treatments together in one calm, professional setting in Sidmouth. Some people arrive with a specific treatment in mind. Others simply want a clear, honest conversation about what might help.

Both are welcome. Every appointment starts with an explanation of what the treatment involves, what it is listed as offering, and what it will cost — so you can decide without pressure. Where something is outside the clinic's scope, you will be told.

Meet Louisa Rees

Careful hands,honest advice.

Louisa is the founder and lead aesthetic practitioner at LR MediClinic. Before moving into aesthetics, she spent 15 years working as an NHS phlebotomist in London. That experience shaped the way she cares for clients today: with a calm, gentle manner, close attention to detail and a strong focus on safety and wellbeing.

Louisa trained in advanced aesthetic medicine at the Derma Institute and holds a Level 7 qualification. She continues to build on her knowledge through ongoing professional development, with a particular interest in helping women feel confident in their skin as it changes with age.

Her approach is personal and considered. Louisa takes time to understand each client's concerns, talk through suitable options and create a treatment plan focused on natural-looking results. For prescription-only treatments, she works exclusively with appropriately qualified prescribers registered with the GMC, GDC or NMC.

Louisa is fully insured and DBS checked, giving clients added reassurance throughout their care.

Consultations & booking

A consultation comes before treatment at LR MediClinic. It is how suitability is assessed and how a plan is agreed — including for injectable, device and laser treatments. The clinic will confirm what is required for the treatment you are interested in.

You do not need to arrive with an answer. Louisa will talk through your concerns and the options that could help, and will say clearly if something is unlikely to give you what you are hoping for. Sometimes the honest answer is to wait, or to see someone else.

Appointments are made through the clinic's official booking platform, by phone, or by email. This website does not show live availability, and nothing is reserved until the clinic confirms your appointment.

You will talk through your concerns, your medical history and any treatments you have had before. Louisa will assess the area, explain suitable options, the costs and the aftercare, and answer your questions. Treatment only goes ahead if you both agree it is appropriate.

No. A consultation is a conversation, not a commitment. You are welcome to go away and think about it.

Bring details of any medication, allergies or medical conditions that could be relevant, and mention previous aesthetic treatments. If something specific is needed, the clinic will tell you when your appointment is confirmed.

Contact the clinic directly as early as you can, and they will explain the options. The clinic confirms its own booking and cancellation terms when your appointment is made.

Treatments

The aim is always a natural-looking result, and treatment plans are built around that. Every person responds differently, so no specific outcome can be guaranteed — and where a result is unlikely, you will be told before you decide.

It depends on the treatment. Some are listed as courses — laser hair removal, for example, typically requires a series of sessions, and Profhilo is listed with a course of two initial treatments. Louisa will explain the expected plan at your consultation.

Sometimes treatments can be combined, and sometimes they are best spaced apart. This is always a clinical decision made by Louisa, based on the treatments involved, your skin and how you heal. Please don't plan a combination yourself without discussing it first.

Preparation & aftercare

Yes. You will be given aftercare advice specific to the treatment you have had, and a way to contact the clinic if you have a question afterwards. Please follow the guidance you are given rather than general advice online — it is written for your treatment.

Not always immediately. Some products — particularly active ingredients — may need to be paused for a while. Louisa will tell you what to avoid and for how long; please follow her guidance for your specific treatment rather than a general rule.

It depends on the treatment. Some allow makeup straight away, others need the skin left clean for a period. Follow the advice you are given on the day — this is confirmed with you individually.

Depending on the treatment, strenuous exercise may need to wait. Ask Louisa at your appointment and follow her guidance, as the advice differs between injectables, peels and device treatments.

Sun and heat exposure can affect healing and results, and sensitivity varies by treatment. Please follow the sun-care advice given for your procedure rather than assuming a set number of days.

Mild redness, swelling or tenderness can be a normal part of settling after some treatments and usually eases. Because it varies by procedure, check the aftercare information you were given and contact the clinic if anything concerns you.

Contact the clinic first and describe what you are experiencing. If symptoms are severe or getting worse — such as difficulty breathing, a spreading rash, severe pain, signs of infection or changes in vision — seek prompt medical assessment without waiting, using NHS 111, your GP or emergency services as appropriate.

A note on advice

Aftercare differs between treatments, and it is confirmed with you individually on the day. Please follow the guidance Louisa gives you rather than general advice found online. Nothing on this page is medical advice or a diagnosis.

If you are worried about symptoms after a treatment, contact the clinic. For severe or worsening symptoms, seek prompt medical assessment — NHS 111, your GP, or emergency services as appropriate.
